ZeroNic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

ZeroNic is an app that helps you track how long you have gone without nicotine and other habits. This policy explains what information the app uses and who can see it.

Your data stays on your phone

ZeroNic has no accounts and no servers. The information you enter, such as quit dates, what you used to spend, your currency, and your settings, is stored only on your device. I (the developer) cannot see it and do not collect it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Face ID / passcode lock

If you turn on the app lock, ZeroNic asks iOS to check your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. The check is done by iOS. The app never receives your biometric data.

Ads

ZeroNic shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and how you interact with ads. In the EU, EEA and UK, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. On iOS, the app asks permission before using the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change your choices at any time in the app's Store screen ("Privacy options") or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

You can remove ads with the "Remove ads" purchase. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technology/partner-sites.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive your payment details or name. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase has been made, so it can unlock what you bought.

Health and well-being information

ZeroNic is not a medical product and does not give medical advice. Health information in the app comes from public sources and is for general information only. The app does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Children

ZeroNic is not intended for children under 13 (and is rated for older users). I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
