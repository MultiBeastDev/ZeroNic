About ZeroNic

Why I built it

Hi, I'm Nick.

I've struggled with nicotine addiction, and every time I tried to quit I looked for an app to track my progress. Almost every one I found locked the useful parts behind a monthly subscription. Paying every month just to see how long I'd gone without nicotine felt wrong, so I decided to build my own.

ZeroNic is the app I wanted: a simple timer for how long you've been free, your milestones, how much money you've saved, and honest information about how your body recovers. You enter your own prices and your own quit dates, and your data stays on your phone.

What you get

  • No subscription. Not now, not later.
  • Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase.
  • Optional add-on packs are one-time purchases too, never recurring.
  • Private by design. No account, and nothing about your progress is sent to me.

Quitting is hard, and slips happen. What matters is that you keep going. If you're struggling, the Support screen in the app links to free, confidential help.
